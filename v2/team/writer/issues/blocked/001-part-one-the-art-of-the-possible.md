# Part one: the art of the possible

Three memos ask for the same opening. Brief 42: *"literally describe, screenshot
features, and basically say: here is what writing a book in 2026 looks like."* Brief
45 adds the reason, and it is a better one than the record had: the same content
serves both audiences at different reading speeds, because *"they still go to the
same steps."*

## What is ready

- the capability register, counted rather than remembered (v0.6.7)
- the founder's Leanpub failure story as source material (v0.6.9)
- the screenshots, which exist: figures 08, 17, 18 and 19 already show the workflow,
  and the earlier claim that they did not was wrong

## What is not

**Which book part one opens.** Brief 45 says *"the first book"*, which in this estate
has meant the frozen first edition; brief 42 asked for it in the making-of. The
reading taken is the making-of. If that is wrong, this is being planned for the wrong
volume, and it is the one thing worth being certain about before writing three
thousand words.
