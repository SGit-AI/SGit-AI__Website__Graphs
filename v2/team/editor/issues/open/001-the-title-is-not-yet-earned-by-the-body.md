# The title is not yet earned by the body

The book is *Creating a Book Using Agentic Workflows*. Its own graph counts
**workflow and workflows six times in 27,002 words** of prose, and every other word
in the founder's account of the idea appears **zero** times: villager, zone, pacing,
maturity, commoditised.

This was known when the rename shipped and was recorded rather than hidden. It is not
a reason to reopen the name. It is the work the name created.

## The plan

Nothing until the founder decides stage 5 of the workflow document. Then, and in this
order, because doing it the other way means writing the vocabulary twice:

1. part one, the art of the possible
2. the vocabulary pass across the existing twelve chapters, in the language part one
   established

The measure is the one the altitude ladder already set for the other book's title:
**the one-paragraph version of this book should not be writable without the word.**
