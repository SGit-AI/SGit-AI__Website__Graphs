# The process that turns a document into its folder of graph materials has no name

Two halves have names and the whole does not: **decomposition** is the deterministic
split, **extraction** is the authored reading, and they are the two-way and one-way
halves of one act.

Four candidates were built from counted estate vocabulary and put to the founder at
stage 5. **The intake** is recommended: it names the thing rather than the activity
and collides with nothing. *The decomposition* is the one to refuse, because
promoting it would make eleven existing uses ambiguous, four of them in frozen
release narration.

Blocked, and correctly so: naming is the founder's call, as the book title was.

**One requirement whichever wins**, and it is the librarian's actual job here: the
name must be recorded where the process is *run*, not only where it was decided, or
it will be a name in a pack that nobody uses.
