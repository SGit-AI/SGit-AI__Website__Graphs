# Brief: map the making-of book

**Asked by** the founder, brief 40, through the change-control plan for the naming question.
**Site version at asking** v0.6.3
**Action** `actions/map-a-book.md`
**Subject** `v2/books/making-a-book/` — *Creating a Book Using Fractal Semantic Graphs*, v0.1.0

## The question

The founder judges the title wrong: *"we are not using the fractal element here… although
it's a graph-like environment, this is more about the workflows that we're creating."*

Before anyone proposes a replacement, establish **what the book actually is**, from the
book itself. The memo asks for exactly this: *"a research project where we map out what is
the book, what is the book about."*

## What may be assumed

Nothing about the founder's judgement being right. The map is evidence; the verdict is the
editor's and the decision is the founder's.

## What is needed

The six items in `actions/map-a-book.md`, with particular weight on two:

- **item 5, what it is not about, tested against its own title** — the direct evidence for
  or against the founder's objection;
- **item 6, the words it uses for itself, counted** — so any replacement title is built
  from the book's own vocabulary rather than from a brainstorm.

## Done test

Every numbered claim cites a chapter and a passage, and every count is reproducible from
the chapter markdown. A reader who disagrees can go and check.
