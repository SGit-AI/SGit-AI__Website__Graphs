# The fan-out is two documents, not twenty-one, and one of them was written here

The pilot's promise was that the machinery would scale to the carried estate without
redesign. At v0.6.12 it has run over **two** documents, and the second was written
in v2 by the same agent that extracted it.

That second one is real evidence for the *machinery* (about 40 lines of change, and
the pilot's output byte-identical afterwards) and **no evidence at all** for the
extraction method, because a document written with its own extraction in mind
extracts well by construction. The estate should not compare yields across a carried
document and a projected one without saying which is which, and now says so on the
document's own page.

## The plan

Extract one genuinely carried source that nobody here wrote, and see what breaks.
*Compatibility Through Connectivity* is the candidate: same February date, same
author, and **zero** uses of *fractal*, so it tests the vocabulary assumptions rather
than confirming them.
