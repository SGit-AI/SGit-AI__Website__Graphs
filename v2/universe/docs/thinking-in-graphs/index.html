The usage maturity model

A source document captures, when possible, the references of where it is being used and quoted, so that the document, and ideally its author, can rate each use of its contents. The rating is a judgement about the USE, never about the user: the same site can carry aligned uses and misaligned ones side by side, and finding a misaligned one is the mechanism working.

LevelMeaningThe test
AlignedA correct use. The use says what the source says: a verbatim quote in its context, or a faithful paraphrase or derivation that the source's author would recognise as their idea.Read the use, then the cited part of the source. Would the source's author say: yes, that is what I said?
StretchedAn ambiguous or loosened use. Recognisably derived from the source, but context was dropped, scope was widened, or a nuance the source insists on was lost. Not false, and not quite what the source says.The author would say: that came from my document, but it is not quite what I meant, or it leaves out something I said mattered.
MisalignedA wrong use. The use claims the source says something it does not: a misquote, an inverted meaning, an attribution the text cannot support.The author would say: that is not what the document says.
UnratedA known use that nobody has judged yet. Recorded so the absence of a rating is visible rather than hidden.None. This is the named absence, awaiting a judgement.

For an agent

This folder is self-contained: source.md is a build-verified byte copy of the frozen original, extraction.json is the layer 1 local graph with gate-verified anchors, and crossrefs.json records the known uses of this document with a signed, dated rating against ../../usage-model.json. If you use this document's material anywhere, the honest move is to add a crossref entry for your use, rated unrated, so the document's steward can judge it. A rating judges the use, never the user.
