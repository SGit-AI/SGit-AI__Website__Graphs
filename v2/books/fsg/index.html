Fractal Semantic Graphs: Meaning Through Connectivity

A node connected to nothing means nothing. What a thing is emerges from the edges traceable from it, and how much you can rely on that meaning is a function of how richly and how independently it is connected. This is the edition that argues that from first principles and from the running system.
