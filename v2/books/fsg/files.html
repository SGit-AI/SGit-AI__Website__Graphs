The files of Fractal Semantic Graphs: Meaning Through Connectivity

v0.2.0 · 5 folders, 64 files. Everything this book is made of and everything the build derived from it, in one explorer. Raw is the exact bytes with minimal formatting; files the build understands also carry their own view — the markdown rendered, book.json as the two-clock changelog and the chapter hashes the version gate reads, the graph index as the chapter table, each chapter's shards as their blocks with uids. The markdown under content/ is the source of truth: every rendered page and the PDF are projections of it.

Where the versions come from. This book is at v0.2.0 and the site is at v0.6.17. Two clocks: the book's moves only when its content moves, the site's on every push. The rules · read the book
graphs.sgit.ai

A reference site about one use of graphs: meaning through connectivity — a node carries no inherent meaning, and what a thing is emerges from the edges traceable from it. Part of the sgit.ai network. All content on this site is released under CC BY 4.0.
