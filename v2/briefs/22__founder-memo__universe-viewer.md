## The questions the agent owes answers on

1. **The freeze mode.** Proposed semantics: a freeze control pins the current visible set; while frozen, tapping a node toggles it in or out of the set, and an expand control adds the neighbours of the selection one step at a time, with a direction choice (incoming, outgoing, both). Is that the intended workflow?
2. **The first weaker-link derivation.** Proposed: two concepts are weakly linked when one claim is about both (dashed, with the count). Alternatives: co-location in a section, or thesaurus adjacency. Which first?
3. **The exhibits edge.** The extraction records `graphs-of-graphs exhibits fractal-principle` (anchored to "At every level, the same principles apply"). Is the complaint about the direction of the fact, or about how the drawing renders direction? If the fact is wrong, the fix is one anchored edit in the extraction.
4. **The subtree meaning.** Today subtree shows the selection plus everything that hangs off it. The memo says "only the nodes that are selected". Should subtree become the frozen-set workflow of instruction 11, replacing the hang-off expansion?
