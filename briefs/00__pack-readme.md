## Tiers used in the manifest

- **Tier 0 (19)** — publishable near-as-is, highest value
- **Tier 1 (57)** — publishable with framing, a rewrite, or a render
- **Tier 2 (9)** — needs de-scoping, a "not adopted" label, or a future-vision banner
- **Tier 3 (4)** — do not publish (real personal data, third-party legal read, commercial material)

## Verification

Every repo path in this pack — including all 89 manifest rows — was checked to exist at v0.33.62. Two filenames were corrected against the repo during preparation.

Numbers for the three already-published graph vaults come from the live pages on sgit.ai, not from the repo. Numbers for repo artefacts come from parsing the documents. The pack never mixes the two.
