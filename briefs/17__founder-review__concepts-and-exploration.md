# Founder review: the concept layer, and exploring the graph from anywhere

**Date:** 22 August 2026 · **Source:** voice memo, transcribed live, reproduced verbatim
below (per the house rule: the founder's voice is source material and is not edited) ·
**Status:** Review **r004**, third round — items 10 and 11, both applied at
[v0.3.20](../admin/versions.html). The altitude ladder gains a concept layer at
[/altitudes/concepts.html](../altitudes/concepts.html) and the graph explorer is rebuilt.
No book content changed.

**What it says, in one paragraph:** the ontology and taxonomy work captured structure but
missed the **concepts** — the definitions themselves, which are "like a dictionary and a
thesaurus at a three-dimensional level". A concept has sideways relations, downward
levels where it is expanded, and upward levels where it is consolidated, so the graph
should be explorable **from any concept** rather than only from the top: if you want to
explore meaning through connectivity, or nodes, or edges, you should be able to enter
there. There is no single centre of gravity but several **peaks** that concepts flow
into, and the stronger ideas are the ones with more connectivity — count what connects to
a node and the strong concepts announce themselves. Ultimately the book should reduce to
a number of facts, claims, assertions, statements and opinions. On the visualisation:
options for where the node text sits (inside a rounded rectangle, wrapped at twenty or
thirty characters, or capped); an option to run the layout until it stabilises or to
raise the iteration count; the ability to take a screenshot **and capture the
configuration that produced it**, so a view can be recreated rather than only kept —
settings, zoom, what was hidden, what was consolidated, and anything moved by hand;
maximise and resize so the graph can have the screen; double-click to expand, select and
consolidate, expand by radius; and edges carrying labels in both directions so paths can
be built from the edge labels, which is "ridiculously powerful".
